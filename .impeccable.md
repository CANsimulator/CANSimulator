## Design Context

### Users
Embedded systems engineers, automotive diagnostics trainees, and students using the simulator in labs, training sessions, or self-study to visualize and understand CAN protocol behavior.

### Brand Personality
Technical, precise, confident. (Assumption due to no explicit guidance.)

### Aesthetic Direction
Instrument-grade technical aesthetic focused on signal visualization. Primary usage expects dark UI with high-contrast accents, but the logo should remain legible in monochrome and on light backgrounds.

### Design Principles
- Signal-first clarity: prioritize readability and technical accuracy.
- Instrument-grade confidence: crisp geometry and engineered forms.
- Scalable recognition: strong silhouette at favicon size and full lockups.
- Modular system: icon, wordmark, and lockup variants for flexible use.
